Packet and word formats

One row per 32-bit word. Byte P0 is sent first and sits in bits [7:0] of every word bus in the design, except the packer's internal word (P0 in [31:24], swapped once in cxp_app_domain) and the register bus (native, first wire byte in [31:24]). Red bytes are K characters, green is the CRC word.
